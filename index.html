Founder note

if the first 10 pieces sell, we keep going.

Hi, I'm Lalitha.

I have ₹20,000, a notes app, and a loud little idea: what if a saree border lived on your jeans? Not in a museum. Not in a wardrobe you open twice a year. On a Tuesday, with sneakers.

I don't have investors. I don't have a warehouse. Honestly, I don't have a clue. What I have is a karigar who is far better than me at this, a pile of handloom, and fifty days.

Some pieces will come out beautiful. Some will come out wrong, and I'll show you those too. Every rupee goes back into the next piece.

If the first 10 sell, we keep going. If they don't, you'll have watched the whole thing happen, and I'll have learned more than any course could teach me.

Either way, thank you for being here.

— Lalitha ♡
