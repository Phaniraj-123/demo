A House Built On Needle, Thread & Craft

Shengai Fashion Academy began as a small tailoring classroom above a Bengaluru textile market. Today it's both a design school and a label — every garment we sell is designed and stitched by our students and in-house karigars, blending regional weaves with contemporary tailoring.

What you see on our Instagram is what leaves our workroom: no mass production, no dropshipping — just pieces made a few hundred at a time.
