Order summary

Made in a home kitchen, shared with yours

craftedby_aj started with one family laddoo recipe and a habit of gifting it to everyone. Today every batch is still made in small quantities, rolled by hand and packed fresh to order.

We use whole cashews, almonds, pistachios, figs and dates, roasted in pure ghee. Nothing is made far ahead, so what reaches you tastes like it was made for you.
