How to apply
  1. Push back cuticles and wipe nails with the alcohol wipe.
  2. Lightly buff the nail surface.
  3. Pick the right size for each finger.
  4. Apply glue or an adhesive tab, press for 15–20 seconds.
How to remove
  1. Soak in warm soapy water for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Gently lift from the sides with the cuticle pusher.
  3. Never pull or peel them off dry.
  4. Clean off residue, store for reuse.

FAQ

 

How do I get my size right?

Measure each nail’s widest point in mm (see the guide in the custom builder), or choose XS / S / M / L. Sania will confirm before painting.

How long do they last?

With glue, usually 1–2 weeks depending on prep and daily use. Adhesive tabs are better for short wear.

Can I reuse them?

Yes. Remove gently with warm soapy water, clean off the glue and store them in the box.

How long does a made-to-order set take?

Timelines depend on design and queue — confirm with Sania when you order.

Do you ship across India?

Ships from Zira, India. Delivery times and rates are confirmed by Sania; free shipping over ₹999 on this site’s checkout.

Returns for custom sets?

Custom sets are made for you, so please check the size and design details before confirming. Ask Sania about issues on arrival.
