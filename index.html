Common questions

Will I have to give up my favourite foods?

No. Plans work with the food you already eat, with portion and timing changes instead of strict bans.

Can I follow a plan if I have a medical condition?

Yes. Share your conditions and medicines in the form so the plan can be adjusted. It supports, and does not replace, your doctor's treatment.

How soon will I see results?

It varies from person to person. Healthy change is gradual, and regular follow-ups keep it on track.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. You can consult and follow your plan from anywhere over call and WhatsApp.
