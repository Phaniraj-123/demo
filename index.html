How to order

1. Pick your bakes

Add items to your cart. Everything is baked fresh, so order 2 to 3 days ahead for cakes.

2. Fill in the checkout

Share your details, the date you need it and whether you want delivery or shipping.

3. Send it on Instagram

We copy your order into a message. Send it to @cakescookies_28 and Ritu confirms the price and payment.

Pan-India shipping

Cookies and rusk ship across India. Shipping cost is added when Ritu confirms your order.
