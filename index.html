Welcome home

Why a home beats a kennel

Dogs are social animals. A noisy kennel can bring on stress, skipped meals and barking fits. A calm home is much closer to what your dog knows.

Boarding at Noah's Arf

  • Lives inside a real home, free to roam
  • Plays with a small group of friendly dogs
  • Meals, walks and naps follow your dog's routine
  • Someone is always around, day and night
  • Daily updates so you can enjoy your trip

Typical kennel boarding

  • Spends many hours alone in a cage or run
  • Constant noise from unfamiliar dogs
  • One fixed schedule for every dog
  • Staff often leave at night
  • Little or no news while you are away

The dog parent guide

Tips that help before any boarding stay, wherever you go.

Vaccinations to keep current
  • Rabies and the core combination vaccine (DHPP)
  • Kennel cough (Bordetella) is a good extra
  • Tick and flea prevention up to date
  • Carry your vaccination card on drop-off
Easing separation anxiety

Keep goodbyes short and calm. Dogs read your mood, so a cheerful exit helps. A short trial stay before a long trip builds confidence. Leave a worn t-shirt that smells like you.

Bangalore weather and your dog

Mild weather is easy, but monsoon brings damp coats, ticks and skin infections. Dry your dog well after walks. Avoid walking on hot pavement at midday in summer, and always keep fresh water available.

Signs of a stressed dog
  • Panting or pacing without heat or exercise
  • Refusing food for over a day
  • Hiding, trembling or excessive licking
  • Unusual clinginess or barking
Foods that are dangerous for dogs

Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol sweeteners, macadamia nuts and cooked bones can all cause serious harm. Share this list with anyone who feeds your dog.

Quick quiz: is it safe for dogs?

Questions we often hear

Will my dog be in a cage?

No. Noah's Arf is kennel-free. Dogs live freely in the home. A crate is only used if your own dog is crate-trained and you ask for it.

How many dogs do you host at once?

We keep the pack small on purpose so every guest gets attention. Ask us on WhatsApp for current availability.

Can you board puppies, seniors or dogs on medication?

Yes, case by case. Tell us about age and health on the booking page and we will confirm we can care for your dog well.

What if my dog gets ill or hurt?

We contact you right away and take your dog to your vet, or the nearest clinic if needed. Please share your vet's details when booking.

Are dogs vaccinated and tested for temperament?

All guests need up-to-date vaccinations. A meet and greet checks that your dog gets along with the pack.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, especially for holidays and long weekends in Bangalore. Book as early as you can.
